06 · Law & GDPR — this is a moat, not a problem

Doctors may not advertise in Greece. They may absolutely have a secretary.

Κώδικας Ιατρικής Δεοντολογίας (Ν. 3418/2005, άρθ. 17–18)

Doctors are forbidden «προσωπική διαφήμιση ή συστηματική δημόσια παρουσία» and may not publish thank-you letters or success stories for advertising. They may keep a website with factual information (άρθ. 18) and inform the public. Dentists have a similar code.

What this means for us: the receptionist, the website, the Business Profile with facts, SMS reminders and patients posting their own Google reviews are all information and service — fully compliant. Paid Google/Meta ads for ιατροί and οδοντίατροι are a grey zone many do anyway; we do not make them the core. We sell ads only to ψυχολόγοι, φυσικοθεραπευτές, διαιτολόγοι, λογοθεραπευτές (not under this code) and to clinics framed as information — and we tell doctors honestly why. That honesty wins trust and makes the «organic patient system» the obvious choice.

GDPR — health data

  • A caller's reason for visiting is health data (Art. 9). The receptionist collects the minimum: name, phone, the slot, and a short free-text reason only if the patient offers it. No ID numbers, no history.
  • Advon = processor, the practice = controller. One signed DPA per client (ours, 2 pages, Greek), listing sub-processors: ElevenLabs (EU data residency setting on), the LLM provider, the SIP provider, the SMS gateway. I draft it; a lawyer reviews it once (€300–500).
  • Audio recording off by default; text transcripts kept 90 days then deleted; patient SMS only for the appointment itself; review requests only to patients the doctor taps «send» for.
  • Professional secrecy (άρθ. 13 ΚΙΔ): confidentiality clause in our contract; access to transcripts only you and me.
  • The practice's privacy page gets a paragraph on the digital receptionist — I add it to every site we host.

EU AI Act, Article 50 (in force since 2 Aug 2026)

Anyone talking to an AI must be told. Our receptionist opens with «Ιατρείο Δρ. Παπαδοπούλου, είμαι η ψηφιακή βοηθός του ιατρείου». That single sentence is the whole obligation — and honestly, patients prefer it to a voicemail. No high-risk classification applies (booking appointments is not medical decision-making), and the agent is forbidden to give medical advice, which keeps it that way.

Why this is a moat: a 19-year-old reseller of an American voice bot will not do the DPA, the Greek privacy page, the medical-code honesty or the EU residency. You will — and you will say so in the meeting.
