4 · How the campaign is set up

Ad accountAdvon Media business account (act 1212936993186625), never the personal one
GoalLeads → website → pixel event Lead (someone sends the form)
Budget€30 a day, one ad set, so all the money learns in one place
Who sees itGreece, age 27–65, Advantage+ audience (Meta finds the doctors and professionals itself)
WhereAdvantage+ placements: Facebook and Instagram feed (4:5), Reels and Stories (9:16)
AdsThe 3 above. Meta gives more money to the one that works best.
RulesChange nothing for the first 7 days. Day 7: turn off any ad with no leads and a cost per click over €1.50. Day 14: judge by cost per lead (under €15 good · €15–25 OK · over €30 change something).

5 · What happens next

  1. You check the landing page preview on your phone. Tell me anything to change.
  2. You say «yes, put it live». That's 1 deploy of advonmedia.com, 15 credits, and the page appears at advonmedia.com/istoselida. Your homepage doesn't change.
  3. I send one test lead through the page to check it reaches your e-mail, Telegram and CRM «From the ads», and that Meta counts the Lead.
  4. You add the files to the draft in Ads Manager (videos + image + texts above), because Meta doesn't let me upload files from here.
  5. You enter the ΑΦΜ and payment in the ad account, then set the budget to €30/day with a spending limit.
  6. Publish. I check days 1–3 (that it's spending and the pixel counts), then day 7 and day 14 by the rules above. You get a report every Monday.
  7. Call every lead within 5–10 minutes. That matters more than anything else.
