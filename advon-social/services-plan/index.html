2Meta (Instagram + Facebook): access without passwords

Never take a password. Three reasons you can say to a client who offers it: it is against Meta's rules, a login from a new device locks the account («suspicious activity»), and if anything ever happens to the account, the person with the password is blamed. The professional way is partner access: the client keeps the account, we get permission to post, and they can remove us with one click the day we stop. Everything we post appears as their page, not as you.

What the client needs to have

  • A Facebook Page for the practice (not only a personal profile).
  • An Instagram professional account (Business or Creator) — a personal one is switched in 2 minutes: Settings → Account type.
  • The Instagram account connected to the Page (Meta Business Suite → Settings → Business assets). Most already have this; if not, we guide them.

No Facebook page? We create one with them in 10 minutes on a call — it is needed for scheduling and later for ads.

What I do (from your Business Portfolio «Advon Media»)

  1. Business settings → Accounts → Pages → Add → «Request access to a Page»I type the client's page name, tick: Content, Community activity, Insights (and Ads only if they take boosting).
  2. Same for Instagram accounts and, if needed, Ad accountsAccounts → Instagram accounts → Add → Request access.
  3. The client gets a notification and presses ApproveIn Facebook or Meta Business Suite. That is all they do. I check «Partners» shows Advon Media with the right tasks.

7Google Ads, explained as simply as possible

Someone in Περιστέρι types «ψυχολόγος Περιστέρι» into Google. Above the map and the normal results there are 2–3 results marked «Χορηγούμενο». Google Ads is how you get there. You do not pay to be shown — you pay only when someone clicks. That click costs roughly 0,50–3€ depending on the profession and the city. The person lands on the client's website (which we built to make people call) and either calls, books on doctoranytime, or leaves.

5€/dayminimum budget we propose = ~150€/month to Google
~1€a click for a psychologist in Athens (dentist ~1,5–2,5€, lawyer ~2–4€, physio ~0,8€)
100–150clicks a month at 5€/day for a psychologist
8–15calls/bookings a month if 6–10% of clicks convert — a good mobile site does

These are estimates from what I know of the Greek market; the real numbers for a specialty + area come from Google's own Keyword Planner inside our Manager account — that is the «δωρεάν έλεγχος περιοχής» on your offer page, and it is real: 20 minutes, no money spent.

What decides if it works

  • The right words. «ψυχολόγος Περιστέρι» yes; «ψυχολόγος μισθός» no. We add «negative» words so the ad never shows for students, jobs, free services, public hospitals.
  • The right area. A radius around the practice (e.g. 6 km) or the municipality — and only people who are physically there, not people «interested in» Athens from abroad.
  • The right hours. Ads run when someone can answer the phone. Evenings and Sunday nights are when people search for help — we keep them on if the site has booking.
  • Measuring. A tiny tag on the website counts every call button tap and booking click that came from an ad. Without it we are blind; with it Google learns who calls and shows the ad to similar people.
  • Patience. The first 2–3 weeks are «learning». Month 2 is cheaper than month 1, month 3 cheaper again — because we cut what did not bring calls.

What we will NOT do (and why)

  • No «Performance Max», no Display banners, no YouTube for a small practice — they burn money on people who were not searching.
  • No «smart» campaigns Google pushes on new accounts — they hide the search words.
  • No spending on your card, ever. Google charges the client's own card; the account belongs to the client.
  • No promises of a number of patients. We promise contacts (calls + bookings), which we can count.
  • No superlatives in ads («ο καλύτερος», «Νο1») — Greek medical advertising rules and Google's health policy.
