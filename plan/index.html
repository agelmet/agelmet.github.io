02 · The three truths (what I think, straight)
Truth 1

Websites alone can never reach 20k net.

20k net ≈ 26–28k revenue. At 130€ a site that is ~150 new sites every month, forever, plus a team to deliver them. Even your ambitious 80/month gives ~10k gross. The site is the hook, not the business.

Truth 2

The money is in the monthly service you don't sell yet.

A psychologist pays 10,83€/month for the site and would pay 150–250€/month for patients (Google Ads, local SEO, GBP, social). 100 clients on 150€ = 15k/month. You already have 220 to ask. This is the whole game for the next 12 months.

Truth 3

You are the bottleneck, by design, and that must flip.

You do support, calls, meetings, QA, pushes. Your highest-ROI hours are closing meetings, selling retainers and hiring. Everything else goes to Claude + one hired operator within 60 days. Not “when I have time” — hiring IS the time.

Think of Advon as a subscription company for Greek professionals whose onboarding gift is a free website — not as a web agency.

Why 20k is realistic in ~8–10 months

You need 100 retainer clients. You will have ~350–400 clients by then. That is a 25–30% attach rate on people who already trust you — and every new client is offered the retainer on the first-draft call. 10 new retainers a month from month 2 gets you there.

Lever 4 · Product

Software margin

The AI receptionist (or the CRM/chat tool you already use) at ~99€/month. Start only after retainers are humming (Q3 2027). It is the bridge from 40k to 100k.

07 · Hiring in Greece — the whole process, step by step

Who to call, what it costs, how not to get burned.

Short version: your accountant (Χιώτης) does all the paperwork; you choose the person and the form of contract. Below are the three legal forms, real 2026 costs, where to find people, and the exact steps.

Form A · Employee (μερική ή πλήρης)

Best for the operator

  • Declared in ΕΡΓΑΝΗ (form Ε3) by the accountant before the first hour of work
  • Minimum wage from 1 Apr 2026: 920€ gross/month full-time (≈772€ net); 4h/day part-time ≈ 460€ gross
  • Employer contributions ≈ 22% on top; 14 salaries a year (Christmas, Easter, holiday)
  • All-in cost: full-time ≈ 1,300€/month, part-time 4h ≈ 650€/month
  • Digital work card (ψηφιακή κάρτα) — accountant sets it up
  • Accounting adds ~30–50€/month for payroll (ΑΠΔ)
Form B · Freelancer (μπλοκάκι)

Best for the caller & specialists

  • They invoice you (τιμολόγιο παροχής υπηρεσιών); no ΕΡΓΑΝΗ
  • Perfect for commission-based callers (e.g. 40€ per booked meeting that shows + 60€ per closed client) and for an ads/design freelancer per project
  • The trap: if the freelancer works mainly for you (1–2 clients, fixed hours) the law treats it as employment and you pay the contributions anyway (ν.4387/2016 άρθ. 39 §9). Full-time people → Form A.
Form C · Subsidised hire (ΔΥΠΑ)

Free money if the timing fits

  • 2026 ΔΥΠΑ programmes: 30+ unemployed → 35–75% of the labour cost paid for 12 months; 18–29 with no experience → up to 1,256€/month; mothers programme up to 1,004€/month
  • Condition: the person must be registered unemployed with ΔΥΠΑ before you hire; you must not have laid anyone off in the previous quarter
  • Apply on the ΔΥΠΑ portal (accountant can file it) → approval → then ΕΡΓΑΝΗ hire
  • Programmes open and close in waves — ask Chiotis which is open the week you hire

Where to find the people

  • kariera.gr, skywalker.gr, jobfind.gr — a paid ad is ~100–200€; LinkedIn free post
  • University career offices (Πάντειο, ΕΚΠΑ, ΟΠΑ, ΑΠΘ) for part-time students — ideal operator profile: final-year marketing/communications, organised, writes perfect Greek
  • Facebook groups «Αγγελίες εργασίας Αθήνα», «Part-time jobs»
  • Your own clients: a message to 10 clients «ψάχνω έναν άνθρωπο για…» — psychologists know organised young people
  • Callers: people who already sold by phone (insurance, telecoms, energy) — post on the same boards with «προμήθεια + bonus»
  • Remote Greek speakers abroad (Germany, UK) — cheaper, no office needed

The hiring process (2 weeks, ~6 hours of your time)

1
Job ad — Claude writes it: «Βοηθός λειτουργίας σε web agency, 4ωρη, remote/Αθήνα, 500€ + bonus». Ask for a 1-minute voice note about why they want it (filters 80%).
2
Screen — Claude ranks CVs; you read 10, call 5 for 15 min.
3
Paid trial (2 h, 30€) — reply to 3 real client messages, QA one draft on a phone, log it in the CRM. You see the real person, not the interview.
4
Offer — send Chiotis: name, ΑΦΜ, ΑΜΚΑ, IBAN, start date, hours, salary. He files ΕΡΓΑΝΗ + contract. Start on a Monday.
5
Onboard — Claude-written manual + screen recordings; 2 weeks shadowing you 1 h/day; from week 3 they own support. 3-month trial period is standard — use it.

Company form — talk to Chiotis in December

You are an ατομική επιχείρηση. Once you have employees and net above roughly 40–50k€/year, an ΙΚΕ usually wins: 22% corporate tax + 5% on dividends instead of the personal scale that climbs to 44%, limited liability, and — critical for Phase 4 — a company you can actually sell or use to buy others. Ask him for the exact crossover number for your case and the cost of the switch (typically 1–2k€ and 2–4 weeks).

11 · The bigger picture — exits and “private equity”

What your company is worth, and what buying companies really means at each stage.

Stage 1 · Advon at 20k net

≈ 0.6–1.0 M€ valuation

Small agencies sell for 2.5–4× annual owner earnings (SDE), more when revenue is recurring and the owner is replaceable. 240k net/year with 100+ subscriptions and a team that runs it ≈ 0.6–1 M€. Without the team and subscriptions, the same revenue is worth ~1×, because the buyer is buying you.

Stage 2 · Advon at 100k net

≈ 4–7 M€ valuation

1.2 M€/year of mostly recurring profit with software inside is a strategic asset for a Greek/EU marketing group or a SaaS roll-up. 4–6× EBITDA is the range. This is a sellable company, not a job.

Stage 3 · You as the buyer

Entrepreneurship through acquisition

“Private equity” at this scale = buying small businesses (100k–1M€ price) with seller financing (the owner is paid over 3–5 years from the business’s own profit), improving them with your machine, and selling at a higher multiple. Targets: ageing-owner web agencies with 300+ hosting clients, local lead-gen businesses, directories. You buy clients, not code.

What a buyer (or a bank) will ask you in 2029

  • ΙΚΕ with clean books for 24+ months (Chiotis)
  • Recurring revenue ≥ 60% of total; churn < 3%/month
  • A GM who runs it; you below 20% of operations
  • Documented systems (you already have CLAUDE.md — that is literally the operations manual)
  • Contracts with clients (even 1-page), consent for marketing use

Why not skip to buying now

Because every acquisition needs cash flow to service the debt and a team to absorb the business. At 20k net you can buy a 300k€ company with 100k down; at 100k net you can buy 1–2 M€ companies. The ladder is the same: recurring revenue → replace yourself → clean entity → buy with the profit. Each phase above exists to climb one rung.
