How the numbers are made

  • Daily closing prices of the S&P 500 index (^GSPC) since 30 Dec 1927, and the official total-return index (^SP500TR) since 1988, from Yahoo Finance. Last close: .
  • “With dividends” means dividends are reinvested, like an accumulating ETF. Before 1988 it is rebuilt from Robert Shiller’s monthly dividend data, so it is a close estimate rather than exact.
  • Euro results convert the money into dollars at that day’s EUR/USD rate and back at today’s rate (). Daily EUR/USD data starts in Dec 2003, so earlier dates work in dollars only.
  • No fees, taxes or withholding are subtracted. A low-cost S&P 500 ETF costs about 0.03–0.10% a year, and the 15% US tax on dividends inside an Irish ETF trims roughly 0.2% a year — real results land a little lower.
  • Past results show what happened, not what will happen. This page is a calculator, not financial advice.
