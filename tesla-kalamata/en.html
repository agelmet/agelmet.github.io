The 5 rules that keep this stress-free
  1. Night before: set the charge limit to 100% (fine for your battery type) and leave full.
  2. One stop each way, in Tripoli, charging to 100% (~40 min). Keep driving in Kalamata under 20 km.
  3. Never drive past a working charger when you are under 35%. Stop, add 15 minutes, carry on.
  4. Morning of the trip: check in the Electrip app that the Tripoli charger shows as working.
  5. Apps installed + card added before leaving: Electrip, as backup PPC blue, nrg incharge, ElpeFuture ChargenGo (see Plan B). Tesla roadside help: 2111 984867.

What to prepare, step by step

Important: you do not use the Tesla app at these chargers. The Tesla app only works at Tesla Superchargers. Here you start charging from the charger company's app (Electrip, ΔΕΗ blue, nrg, ElpeFuture).

From today (once)

  1. Electrip with your card: done ✓
  2. In Electrip, look for Auto Charge: if it's there, turn it on and add the car, so charging starts by itself when you plug in.
  3. As backup (3 minutes): download PPC blue (ΔΕΗ blue's app), nrg incharge and ElpeFuture ChargenGo, with your card. Only needed for Plan B.
  4. Save Tesla roadside help in your phone: 2111 984867.

The night before

  1. In the car: Controls → Charging → charge limit 100%, and charge at home.
  2. Open this page once on your phone.

The morning you leave

  1. Check you're at 100%.
  2. In the Electrip app: does "E-Drive Cafe" show as working?
  3. If it's cold, warm the cabin from the Tesla app while still plugged in (power comes from the house, not the battery).
  4. Set the car's navigation: Kapandriti → Electrip Tripoli (Σπάρτης 60–62) → Kalamata.

On the road

  1. Drive normally (110–120 km/h helps a lot).
  2. If the battery drops under 45% before Tripoli (not expected): Plan B, ΣΕΑ Σπαθοβουνίου.
  3. ~20–30 min before Tripoli: Controls → Charging → "Precondition battery" (if you see it). It warms the battery so it charges faster. If it isn't there, no problem.
  4. Open the Electrip app and check a spot is free. If not, go to Plan B.

At the charger

  1. Park and open the charge port (press it or use the screen).
  2. Plug the charger's CCS cable into the car.
  3. In the Electrip app: pick the charger number shown on the machine → Start.
  4. Check the % rises on the car's screen. If nothing in 1–2 min: unplug, plug back in, press start again. Still nothing: another spot or Plan B.
  5. At 100%: Stop in the app, unplug, carry on.

Good to know
  • There are no Tesla Superchargers on this route (the nearest are near Patras). All stops here are other companies' fast chargers; your Model 3 plugs in normally (CCS).
  • Rest-stop charger power comes from a published list and can change. The pins at rest stops (ΣΕΑ) are approximate; the Google Maps links search the exact place.
  • Your car's own range estimate will drop faster than the km shown when you climb towards Tripoli and recover on the way down to Kalamata. That's normal.
  • Set the next charger as a stop in the car's navigation so the battery warms up and charges faster.
