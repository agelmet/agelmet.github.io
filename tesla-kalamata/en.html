The 5 rules that keep this stress-free
  1. Night before: set the charge limit to 100% (fine for your battery type) and leave full.
  2. Charge twice going (Tripoli + Αρφαρά, just before Kalamata) and once coming back (Tripoli). That way you leave Kalamata nearly full.
  3. Never drive past a working charger when you are under 35%. Stop, add 15 minutes, carry on.
  4. Morning of the trip: open PlugShare and check Electrip Tripoli and ΣΕΑ Αρφαρών show as working.
  5. Apps installed + card added before leaving: Electrip, nrg incharge, ΔΕΗ blue, ElpeFuture. Tesla roadside help: 2111 984867.

What to prepare, step by step

Important: you do not use the Tesla app at these chargers. The Tesla app only works at Tesla Superchargers. Here you start charging from the charger company's app (Electrip, ΔΕΗ blue, nrg, ElpeFuture).

From today (once)

  1. Download Electrip, ΔΕΗ blue, nrg incharge, ElpeFuture and PlugShare.
  2. Make an account in each and add your card. Not at the charger in the cold.
  3. In Electrip, look for Auto Charge: if it's there, turn it on and add the car, so charging starts by itself when you plug in.
  4. Test charge near home at a ΔΕΗ blue or nrg charger for 5–10 minutes, so the first time isn't in Tripoli.
  5. Save Tesla roadside help in your phone: 2111 984867.

The night before

  1. In the car: Controls → Charging → charge limit 100%, and charge at home.
  2. Open this page once on your phone.

The morning you leave

  1. Check you're at 100%.
  2. In PlugShare or the apps: are Electrip Tripoli and ΣΕΑ Αρφαρών working?
  3. If it's cold, warm the cabin from the Tesla app while still plugged in (power comes from the house, not the battery).
  4. Set the car's navigation: Kapandriti → Electrip Tripoli (Σπάρτης 60–62) → Kalamata.

On the road

  1. Drive normally (110–120 km/h helps a lot).
  2. At ΣΕΑ Σπαθοβουνίου: under 50%? Stop 15 min. Otherwise carry on.
  3. ~20–30 min before Tripoli: Controls → Charging → "Precondition battery" (if you see it). It warms the battery so it charges faster. If it isn't there, no problem.
  4. Open the Electrip app and check a spot is free. If not, go to Plan B.

At the charger

  1. Park and open the charge port (press it or use the screen).
  2. Plug the charger's CCS cable into the car.
  3. In the Electrip app: pick the charger number shown on the machine → Start.
  4. Check the % rises on the car's screen. If nothing in 1–2 min: unplug, plug back in, press start again. Still nothing: another spot or Plan B.
  5. At 80% (going) or 95% (coming back): Stop in the app, unplug, carry on.
  6. Same at ΣΕΑ Αρφαρών (up to 100%).

If something goes wrong: going

A
Tripoli · Electrip 400 kW (Σπάρτης 60–62)

Charge to 80% (~25 min). On the side of town facing Kalamata.

B
Tripoli · ΔΕΗ Blue 300 kW (ΑΒ Ναυπλίου 39) or nrg 180 kW (Shell Ναυπλίου 56)

Electrip busy or broken? Both are 3 minutes away. Same plan: charge to 80%.

C
All of Tripoli down → ΣΕΑ Αρφαρών 360 kW (towards Kalamata)

Only if you have 30% or more when you leave Tripoli. It's ~65 km, mostly downhill, so you arrive with ~10–20%. Under 30%: take any working charger in Tripoli (even a slow one) up to 40% first.

✓
Early safety valve · ΣΕΑ Σπαθοβουνίου (towards Kalamata, 50 kW)

~55 km before Tripoli. If you are under 50% there, stop 15 min (adds ~15%). Then Tripoli is relaxed.

!
Αρφαρά top-up broken?

You'll still reach Kalamata with ~55%. Use the 50 kW charger at ΣΕΑ Αρφαρών on the way back (other side of the road, 25 km after Kalamata), 30 min to ~80%. Or plan E below.

E
Emergency · ElpeFuture 150 kW, ΕΚΟ Ηρώων Πολυτεχνείου, Καλαμάτα

Public charger inside Kalamata. Not on your plan, but there if you ever need it.

Good to know
  • There are no Tesla Superchargers on this route (the nearest are near Patras). All stops here are other companies' fast chargers; your Model 3 plugs in normally (CCS).
  • Rest-stop charger power comes from a published list and can change. The pins at rest stops (ΣΕΑ) are approximate; the Google Maps links search the exact place.
  • Your car's own range estimate will drop faster than the km shown when you climb towards Tripoli and recover on the way down to Kalamata. That's normal.
  • Set the next charger as a stop in the car's navigation so the battery warms up and charges faster.
